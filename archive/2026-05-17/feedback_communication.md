- **No emojis** unless explicitly requested
- **No running commentary** on what I'm about to do — state the action, do it, report results
- **No recap summaries** at end of turn — one or two sentences max if needed
- **No narrative of internal deliberation** — focus on results and decisions
- **Be concise** — a clear sentence beats a paragraph
- **Default to no code comments** — only explain WHY, not WHAT

**Why**: The user reads fast and values information density. Extra fluff wastes their time.

**How to apply**: In every response, cut to the essential information. Don't explain what you're going to do before doing it. Don't summarize what just happened unless there's a genuinely non-obvious takeaway.
